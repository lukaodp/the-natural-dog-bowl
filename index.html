GOOD TO KNOW

A few clear answers.

What do I get with the Complete Plan?

All 100 recipes (50 cooked, 50 raw), the traffic-light food guide and the portion calculator.

What is the difference with the Basic Plan?

The Basic Plan has the 50 raw recipes only. The Complete Plan adds the 50 cooked recipes, the food guide and the calculator.

Do I need to cook?

No. Half the recipes are raw, half are cooked. Pick what suits your dog and your routine.

What is the bonus portion calculator?

A bonus included in the Complete Plan. Enter weight, life stage and activity to get a daily range in grams, split into two meals. Weigh it on a kitchen scale and adjust to your dog's body condition.

Can I read it on my phone?

Yes. It is a PDF you can open on a phone, tablet or computer. You get access right after payment.

Are bone-in recipes right for every dog?

No. Raw meaty bones need supervision and are not suitable for every dog. Never feed cooked bones.

Is this veterinary advice?

No. The recipes and portions are educational starting points. Talk to your vet about puppies, senior dogs and any health condition.
